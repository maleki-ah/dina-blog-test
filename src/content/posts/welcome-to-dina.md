**DINA LAB — Distributed Infrastructure for NextGen Applications** — is a research lab working at the intersection of intelligent systems and modern distributed infrastructure.

We study how next-generation applications can be designed, deployed and coordinated across cloud, edge, serverless and federated environments — efficiently, privately and at scale.

## What we work on

Our research spans eight vectors, from model training to runtime execution:

- Large language models and federated LLM (FedLLM)
- Federated learning and adaptive client selection
- Serverless platforms and FaaS scheduling
- Edge computing and self-healing edge systems
- Cloud auto-scaling on Kubernetes
- Software systems and automated refactoring

## Who we are

The lab is directed by **Dr. Mehrdad Ashtiani**, alongside core research leads and researchers working across systems, AI and distributed computing. Meet everyone on the [people page](/people/).

## Join the conversation

We run weekly **DINA Talks** where members present and review current research. Students interested in distributed systems, cloud infrastructure and intelligent applications are welcome to reach out through [GitHub](https://github.com/DINA-Lab-IUST).
